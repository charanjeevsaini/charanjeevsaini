## Science facts presented (all checked)

1. Refraction is the bending of light when it passes from one transparent material into another (at a slant).
2. Light travels in straight lines within one material.
3. Speed of light: air ≈ 3 lakh km/s (3×10⁸ m/s); water ≈ ¾ of that (≈ 2.25 lakh km/s, n ≈ 1.33); glass ≈ ⅔ (≈ 2 lakh km/s, n ≈ 1.5).
4. A material in which light travels slower is *optically denser*.
5. Cause of bending: the part of the beam that enters the slower material first slows first, so the beam turns (toy car onto carpet: the wheel that hits the carpet first slows, the car turns towards the normal).
6. The normal is an imaginary line at 90° to the surface where light hits.
7. Faster → slower (air → water): bends towards the normal. Slower → faster (water → air): bends away from the normal.
8. Light travelling along the normal does not bend (it still changes speed).
9. Broken pencil: light from the submerged part bends away from the normal leaving water; the eye traces rays back in straight lines, so the submerged part appears raised/shifted.
10. Pools look shallower than they are (same reason; apparent depth ≈ real depth ÷ 1.33 when viewed from above).
11. Lenses (spectacles, magnifying glasses) work by refraction.
12. Stars twinkle because starlight is refracted by moving air layers of changing temperature/density.
13. Diagram geometry uses Snell's law, n(water)=1.33: 50° in air ↔ 35° in water; car analogy uses speed ratio 0.75.
