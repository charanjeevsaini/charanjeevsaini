What we manufacture

Two product lines, built for demanding assemblies

Every part is cold-headed and finished in-house, from raw wire to a component ready for your line — in copper, silver alloy, aluminum, mild steel and bimetal/trimetal constructions.

Metal Rivets

Semi Tubular, Trimmed, Straight Head, Double Head Shank, Formed and Copper Rivets — precision cold-headed for structural and wiring-harness use.

Contact Rivets
