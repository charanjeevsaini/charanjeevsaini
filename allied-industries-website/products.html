Metal rivets

Six rivet constructions, one cold-heading process

Manufactured in ETP copper, silver alloys, pure silver, mild steel and aluminium, to national and international quality standards.

Semi Tubular Rivets

Manufactured with utmost precision and accuracy. Available in ETP copper, silver alloys, pure silver, mild steel and aluminum, to your specified head and shank dimensions.

Trimmed Rivets

Produced for head shapes other than round, with the precision and accuracy needed for parts that must key into a specific orientation.

Straight Head Rivets

Produced with maximum contact area, particularly suited to wiring harness applications where consistent contact surface matters most.

Double Head & Double Shank Rivets

Produced to the very close tolerances given in customer drawings — for assemblies that need two defined head or shank faces.

Formed Rivets

Designed for head shapes other than round, produced to the close tolerances customers provide for specialized fittings.

Copper Rivets

Made using ETP copper wires as per customer specifications — a wide assortment sized to your application's challenging requirements.

Contact rivets

Electrical contacts, soft shank and hardened head

Cold-headed from wire so the shank stays soft for riveting while the head work-hardens. Shank diameter is held in proportion to head size to avoid wrinkling during forging.

Trimetal Contact Rivets

An economical alternative to solid silver contacts, without compromising on quality. We manufacture Trimetal Contact Rivets with an AgCdO / AgNi / Ag999 layer bonded to ETP Copper on both sides.

AgCdOAgNiAg999

Weldable Button Contact Rivets

Projection-weldable contacts with an AgCdO / AgNi layer on a Nickel, CuNi or Steel base — built to fuse directly onto your terminal or busbar.

AgCdOAgNiNi / CuNi / Steel base

Bimetal Contact Rivets

The contact layer is normally half the thickness of the head; the ratio can be varied within practical limits to suit specific requirements. Contact layer available in AgCdO, AgNi, AgSnO, Ag999 and other silver alloys.

AgCdOAgNiAgSnOAg999

Disc Contact Rivets

Disc-type contacts available in all alloy compositions as per customer specification — sized to the switching current and mechanical mounting your part requires.

FinishWhat it buys youWatch out forTypical use

ZincProtects steel galvanically even where scratched; very cheapNot a contact surface; white corrosion in humid storageStructural steel rivets, mechanical fasteners
