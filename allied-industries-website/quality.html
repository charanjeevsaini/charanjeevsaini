What this means on the shop floor

Quality checked before it becomes your problem

Incoming material checks

Raw wire and alloy stock is sourced from certified vendors and inspected by our quality controllers before it reaches production.

In-process dimensional testing

Head diameter, shank length and layer thickness are checked against drawing tolerances throughout the production run, not only at the end.

Documented traceability

Our IATF 16949-aligned process keeps records suited to automotive supplier audits — useful when you need to trace a batch back to source.

We can share current certification copies and quality documentation on request.
