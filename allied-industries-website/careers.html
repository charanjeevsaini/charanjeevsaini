Careers

Work on parts that end up in vehicles millions of people drive.

We hire across production, quality control, R&D and administration at our Mayapuri facility. Openings vary by season — send your CV and we'll reach out when a role fits.

Roles we typically hire for

  • Production & cold-heading machine operators
  • Quality control & dimensional inspection
  • R&D / tooling
  • Warehousing & dispatch
  • Accounts & administration
  • Sales & client coordination

Send your CV using the form and mention the area you're interested in — we'll get in touch if there's a fit, now or in an upcoming opening.
