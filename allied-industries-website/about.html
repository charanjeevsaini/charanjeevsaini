About Allied Industries

Twenty-seven years of holding tolerance, one rivet at a time.

Established in 1998 at Delhi, India, Allied Industries has grown into a recognized manufacturer, exporter and supplier of a premium quality range of Metal Rivets and Contact Rivets — precision engineered from the best available raw material, on modern cold-heading equipment, to the dimensions our clients specify.

Our product range includes Bimetal Contact Rivets, Trimetal Contact Rivets, Semi Tubular Rivets, Trimmed Rivets, Straight Head Rivets, Special Double Head & Double Shank Rivets, Formed Rivets and Copper Rivets — used extensively in wiring harnesses, switchgear and electrical assemblies across the automotive and electrical component industries.

How we are set up

How the work is organized

To keep quality consistent at volume, our facility is divided into dedicated sub-units — production, R&D, in-house quality testing, warehousing & packaging, and after-sales support — each staffed by professionals with deep, hands-on experience in their area. These teams work in close coordination with client engineering teams so that finished parts match drawing requirements on the first run, not the third.

Raw material is sourced from certified vendors and examined by our quality controllers before it ever reaches the shop floor, so defects are caught before they become finished parts. Completed goods are stored by category and label in a dedicated warehousing unit, then packed for transit in material that keeps them free of damage — a detail that matters as much for a 500-piece sample order as it does for a container shipment.

Infrastructure

A facility built around the whole production cycle

Manufacturing, distribution, quality control, warehousing & packaging, and sales & marketing sit inside one facility in Mayapuri Industrial Area — so a specification change doesn't have to travel between vendors.

01 / Production

Cold heading & forming

Lathe, welding and cutting machines convert wire stock into rivets and contacts to head diameter, shank length and profile tolerances.

02 / R&D

Drawing to production

New dimensions, alloy layers and head profiles are proven out against customer drawings before a full run is committed.

03 / Quality testing

In-house inspection

Dimensional and material checks run under our ISO 9001:2015 & IATF 16949:2016 quality management system.

04 / Warehousing

Sorted, labeled, protected

Finished goods are organized by category for fast, accurate retrieval and packed to survive transit.

05 / After-sales

Still on the line after delivery

Our team stays available for specification queries, re-orders and feedback once parts are in your assembly.

Who we serve

Trusted by India's automotive and electrical manufacturers

Our contact rivets and metal rivets go into wiring harnesses, switches and electrical assemblies built by some of the country's largest two-wheeler, passenger-vehicle and component manufacturers — including TVS, Honda, Maruti Suzuki, Hero, Spark Minda, Jyoti Ltd, U-Shin, Gilard and Pavna Group.

That client list is also why our quality system isn't optional: automotive electrical components carry close tolerances and zero-defect expectations, and our IATF 16949:2016 certified process is built to meet them consistently, order after order.
